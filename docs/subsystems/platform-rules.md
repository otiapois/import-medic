# Subsystem: Platform Import Profiles (`src/core/profiles/`)

## Shopify products

ImportMedic requires `Handle` and `Title` for its combined product and variant workflow. It recognizes current Shopify headings including `URL handle`, `Description`, `SKU`, `Price`, `Compare-at price`, `Published on online store`, and `Product image URL`, alongside legacy headings. Known aliases are mapped to the supported legacy headings. Other columns are retained. Shopify asks for UTF-8 with LF record separators and supports legacy headings. [Shopify product CSV format](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/products/import-export/using-csv)

- Normalize a handle such as `My Product 01!` to `my-product-01` for the contiguous records that share that original handle. A new product requires a title. Immediately following variant and image records with the same handle may have a blank title.
- Block export if distinct source handles would become the same handle or if records for a product are split into noncontiguous groups. Fix the source grouping or choose distinct handles for colliding products.
- Normalize nonnegative decimal prices and compare-at prices, including the legacy `Variant Compare-at Price` heading. Normalize published values, product status, and spacing around valid HTTP(S) product image URLs. Check inventory quantities as signed whole numbers. A blank quantity in a present quantity column with a `shopify` tracker requires review. Preserve invalid values for review, including duplicate nonempty SKUs with each affected line listed. [Shopify import error guide](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/products/import-export/common-import-issues)
- Pad short records only when the omitted trailing headings are known optional fields. Extra cells and unsafe short records block export.

The separate supplier comparison accepts opt-in nonnegative whole-number quantity updates, using the Shopify product CSV inventory column for single-location stores. The single-file repair profile's signed integer validation applies to reviewing an existing Shopify export. Multi-location stock belongs in Shopify's inventory CSV workflow. [Supplier comparison contract](shopify-price-diff.md) · [Shopify product CSV format](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/products/import-export/using-csv) · [Shopify inventory CSV](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/inventory-and-locations/setup/inventory-csv)

## QuickBooks Online bank transactions

- 3-column output uses `Date,Description,Amount`. Positive amounts are deposits and negative amounts are payments.
- 4-column output uses `Date,Description,Credit,Debit`. The Intuit example puts payments in `Credit` and deposits in `Debit`, with an unsigned amount in one cell and the other cell blank. The generated bytes are checked against that convention. Confirm Money Spent and Money Received column mapping in the QuickBooks import screen. [Intuit CSV preparation guide](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/learn-support/en-uk/help-article/bank-transactions/prepare-csv-file-bank-upload-quickbooks/L4BjLWckq_GB_en_GB)
- Source headings `Withdrawal` and `Money Out` identify payments. `Deposit` and `Money In` identify deposits. Source headings such as `Credit`/`Debit` and `Credit Amount`/`Debit Amount` require the `bank` or `intuit` source convention. `auto` blocks export until the source directions are known. Conflicting source amount layouts also block export.
- Accounting parentheses and clear currency formatting are normalized without floating-point rounding. A single comma with no decimal point stays in review, even when a currency symbol is present. An unsigned split amount that is negative or conflicts with the other amount blocks export. A zero beside a valid split amount is cleared. A zero as the transaction's only amount blocks export, including in the signed layout.
- If the source includes `Currency` or `Currency Code`, bank export checks that retained rows use a single currency and that any amount symbol agrees with the row currency. It records the projection of the currency column in the audit because the bank layouts have no currency field. Without that column, differing currency symbols in the amount fields still require review before export. When multiple explicit currencies are identified, the bank CSV is held and the review lists the counts and physical source lines for each currency.
- Known leading statement metadata, trailing numeric bank summaries, and blank records are removed with original line numbers and raw text. Other source columns omitted by the selected schema are listed in the changes.
- Dates such as `2026-11-24`, `24-Nov-2026`, and unambiguous slash dates are accepted. Slash dates with two valid interpretations use the selected source date order or a consistent unambiguous clue elsewhere in the file. Otherwise export remains blocked until the user chooses a date order or edits the affected dates. Output uses `MM/DD/YYYY` by default and `DD/MM/YYYY` when the user explicitly selects DMY. Invalid calendar dates and missing dates stay visible for review.
- Description controls and repeated spacing are normalized. When an unresolved amount cannot be represented in the selected layout, export is blocked until it is repaired or the layout changes.

## Stripe and PayPal transactions

- Accept itemized Stripe Balance or Payout report headings and PayPal Activity headings with explicit Net and Currency fields. Stripe Payments summaries that omit Net require a source re-export. Accounting output uses `Date,Description,Gross,Fee,Net,Currency,Reference ID`. Values are retained from the source. The engine never calculates a substitute for a missing Net amount. [Stripe Balance report](https://docs.stripe.com/reports/balance) · [Stripe Payout reconciliation](https://docs.stripe.com/reports/payout-reconciliation)
- Reconcile Gross, Fee, and Net with exact integer arithmetic. Cent-valued rows are checked in cents, and any extra source decimal precision is preserved and compared exactly. Stripe deduction reports use `Gross − Fee = Net`, including a negative refunded fee when supplied. PayPal Activity uses `Gross + Fee = Net` with the signed fee movement from that line. A discrepancy raises `reconciliation-mismatch` on the physical source line and holds both accounting and bank export formats. The review displays each amount and the expected Net. The calculation leaves the source amounts available for review without auto-correcting them. [Stripe balance transaction amount fields](https://docs.stripe.com/api/balance_transactions/object)
- Recognized native headings determine the fee convention. Generic accounting headers no longer identify the provider. Set **Fee convention** to the confirmed source convention when reopening a generic or previously exported accounting file, including PayPal signed movement for a PayPal accounting export. The local roundtrip carries the source convention into its validation of the canonical accounting output and retains signed fees.
- Normalize Stripe timestamp strings to their stated calendar day and PayPal slash dates according to the selected or inferred order. Accounting output uses `YYYY-MM-DD`. It retains distinct currencies and zero amounts.
- QuickBooks output maps the original Net to signed Amount. It blocks export for invalid dates, missing or invalid amounts and currency, conflicting currency symbols, unreconciled amounts, zero Net, or multiple explicit currencies. Review groups each identified currency's physical source lines. Transactions stay in the preview without currency conversion.
- PayPal `Balance Impact = Memo` records are removed only when the option is enabled. Removed rows retain original line numbers and raw text. If kept, they remain in the accounting output and prevent a bank export since they do not move the balance. When the source supplies a Balance Impact field, a bank export also requires positive Net for Credit, negative Net for Debit, and review for an unknown or conflicting impact. Currency checks use the retained transactions. [PayPal Activity Download report](https://developer.paypal.com/reports/activity-download/)
